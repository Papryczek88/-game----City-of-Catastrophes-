CITY OF CATASTROPHES 7.0 – HOW TO PLAY

Welcome to City of Catastrophes! Your goal is to build a successful city, earn money, attract residents and survive natural disasters.

1. STARTING THE GAME

1. Download and extract City_of_Catastrophes_7_C_CPP_EN.zip.
2. Open the project folder.
3. Compile the game:

gcc -std=c11 -c engine.c -o engine.o
g++ -std=c++17 main.cpp engine.o -o CityOfCatastrophes.exe

4. Run the game:

.\CityOfCatastrophes.exe

2. YOUR MAIN OBJECTIVE

Build and manage a city on a 4 × 4 map.

You need to:
- Earn money.
- Build houses and other buildings.
- Increase your population.
- Improve your city's attractiveness.
- Earn points.
- Protect your city from disasters.

3. UNDERSTANDING CITY STATISTICS

Money – Used to construct and repair buildings.
Population – Number of people living in your city.
Attractiveness – How attractive your city is.
Points – Your progress and score.
Disaster Shields – Protective devices that reduce disaster damage.

Keep an eye on your money. If you spend everything, you may struggle to develop your city.

4. BUILDINGS

House – Provides residents.
Apartment Block – Provides more residents.
Apartment – A larger residential building.
Park – Improves attractiveness.
Restaurant – Supports city attractiveness and development.
Farm – Supports the city's economy.
Factory – Helps generate income.

Each building has its own construction cost, construction time and benefits.

5. CONSTRUCTING BUILDINGS

1. Open the building menu.
2. Select the building you want.
3. Choose an available map location.
4. Confirm construction.
5. Wait for construction to finish.

Buildings may require several in-game days to complete.

6. HOW TIME WORKS

The game uses in-game days.

As days pass:
- Construction progresses.
- Your economy develops.
- You can receive income.
- Random disasters may occur.

Plan your construction carefully.

7. REPAIRS

Buildings can become damaged.

1. Select the repair option.
2. Choose the damaged building.
3. Pay the repair cost.
4. Restore its condition.

Repairing buildings helps protect your city's development.

8. NATURAL DISASTERS

Your city can experience different disasters:

Flood – Can damage buildings.
Earthquake – Can damage structures.
Meteor – Can cause serious destruction.
Hurricane – Can damage parts of your city.
Fire – Can damage buildings.
Factory Accident – Can affect industrial areas.

Disasters are partly random, so always keep some money available for repairs.

9. DISASTER SHIELDS

Disaster Shields help protect your city.

Their purpose is to reduce the damage caused by disasters.

They do not necessarily prevent all damage, so you should still prepare for emergencies.

10. TIPS FOR BEGINNERS

1. Start by building residential buildings.
2. Develop your economy before spending too much.
3. Build parks to improve attractiveness.
4. Use farms and factories to support your finances.
5. Keep money available for repairs.
6. Do not ignore damaged buildings.
7. Expand your city gradually.
8. Prepare for unexpected disasters.

11. YOUR WINNING STRATEGY

STEP 1 – START SMALL
Build a few houses and establish your population.

STEP 2 – DEVELOP YOUR ECONOMY
Add buildings that help generate income.

STEP 3 – IMPROVE YOUR CITY
Add parks and other attractive buildings.

STEP 4 – PROTECT YOUR INVESTMENT
Use Disaster Shields and repair damaged buildings.

STEP 5 – KEEP EXPANDING
Use your income to develop the remaining map.

GOOD LUCK, MAYOR!

Your city is waiting for you. Build wisely, manage your money and survive the catastrophes!
