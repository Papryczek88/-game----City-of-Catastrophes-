# CITY OF CATASTROPHES 10.0 – HOW TO PLAY

## Welcome to City of Catastrophes!

City of Catastrophes is a terminal-based city-building and management game. Build your city, earn money, attract residents, develop your economy and survive natural disasters.

Version 10.0 includes three language implementations:
- C
- C++
- Rust

It also includes an optional NASM Assembly module for building income calculations.

---

## 1. STARTING THE GAME

### Requirements

- GCC – C compiler
- G++ – C++ compiler
- Rust and Cargo – for the Rust version
- NASM – optional, for Assembly integration

### Windows – C version

Open PowerShell in the project directory:

```powershell
cd Gra-v9\C
gcc -std=c11 main.c engine.c -o CityOfCatastrophes.exe
.\CityOfCatastrophes.exe
```

### Windows – C++ version

```powershell
cd Gra-v9\Cpp
gcc -std=c11 -c engine.c -o engine.o
g++ -std=c++17 main.cpp engine.o -o CityOfCatastrophes.exe
.\CityOfCatastrophes.exe
```

### Rust version

```powershell
cd Gra-v9\Rust
cargo run --release
```

### Optional Assembly version

In the C or Cpp directory, run:

```powershell
.\build-asm-windows.bat
```

This requires NASM, GCC and G++ to be available in PATH.

---

## 2. YOUR MAIN OBJECTIVE

Build and manage a successful city on a 4 × 4 map.

Your objectives are to:

- Earn money.
- Construct buildings.
- Increase your population.
- Improve attractiveness.
- Earn points.
- Protect buildings from disasters.
- Repair damaged structures.
- Develop your city efficiently.

There are 16 available map tiles.

---

## 3. STARTING CONDITIONS

Every new game starts with:

| Statistic | Starting value |
|---|---:|
| Money | 500 |
| Day | 1 |
| Population | 0 |
| Attractiveness | 0 |
| Points | 0 |
| Disaster Shields | 0 |

Manage your starting money carefully. Every building and protective shield costs money.

---

## 4. UNDERSTANDING CITY STATISTICS

**Money** – Used to construct buildings, purchase shields and repair structures.

**Population** – The number of residents living in your city.

**Attractiveness** – Represents how attractive your city is.

**Points** – Your city development score.

**Disaster Shields** – Protective items that reduce damage from one disaster.

**Day** – The current in-game day.

**HP** – The condition of a building. Buildings have up to 100 HP.

---

## 5. BUILDINGS

There are seven available building types.

| Building | Cost | Residents | Income/day | Attractiveness | Construction |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| House | 50 | 5 | 0 | 0 | 1 day |
| Apartment Block | 120 | 20 | 1 | 0 | 3 days |
| Luxury Apartment | 200 | 40 | 2 | +10 | 5 days |
| Park | 80 | 0 | 1 | +15 | 2 days |
| Restaurant | 150 | 5 | 5 | +20 | 3 days |
| Farm | 60 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 2 days |
| Factory | 300 | 10 | 10 | -5 | 7 days |

Building income depends on its current HP. Damaged buildings generate less income.

---

## 6. CONSTRUCTING BUILDINGS

1. Open the main menu.
2. Select **Buy a building**.
3. Choose a building using the Up and Down arrow keys.
4. Press Enter.
5. Select an empty tile on the map.
6. Press Enter to confirm the location.
7. Wait for construction to finish.

Construction progresses when you advance the in-game day.

A building under construction is marked with `#`.

---

## 7. HOW TIME WORKS

The game uses an in-game calendar.

Select **Next day** to advance time by one day.

As time passes:

- Buildings generate income.
- Construction progresses.
- Completed buildings add residents and attractiveness.
- Your points increase when construction is completed.

Some disasters can also advance the calendar by several days.

---

## 8. REPAIRS

Disasters can damage buildings.

To repair a building:

1. Select **Repair a building (50)**.
2. Choose a damaged building.
3. Press Enter to confirm.
4. Pay 50 money.
5. Restore 25 HP.

A building cannot exceed 100 HP.

Repairing buildings is important because damaged buildings generate less income.

---

## 9. NATURAL DISASTERS

The game includes six disaster types:

- **Flood** – Can damage buildings.
- **Earthquake** – Can damage structures.
- **Meteor** – Causes serious destruction.
- **Hurricane** – Can damage buildings.
- **Fire** – Can damage buildings.
- **Factory Accident** – Can occur when your city has a factory.

Disasters can reduce building HP and destroy buildings.

To trigger a disaster manually:

1. Select **Cause a disaster**.
2. Confirm your decision.
3. The game randomly selects a disaster.
4. Check your buildings and repair damaged structures.

Disasters award 30 points.

---

## 10. DISASTER SHIELDS

Disaster Shields cost 200 money each.

To purchase one:

1. Select **Buy disaster shield (200)**.
2. Confirm the purchase.

Each shield protects against one disaster by reducing its damage by 50%.

Shields are consumed when used.

They do not completely prevent damage, so keep money available for repairs.

---

## 11. CONTROLS

The game is controlled entirely through the keyboard.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Up Arrow / W | Move up |
| Down Arrow / S | Move down |
| Left Arrow / A | Move left |
| Right Arrow / D | Move right |
| Enter / Space | Confirm |
| Esc | Go back or exit a menu |

Use the arrow keys to navigate menus and select map tiles.

---

## 12. TIPS FOR BEGINNERS

1. Start by constructing houses.
2. Build farms and restaurants to support your economy.
3. Use parks to improve attractiveness.
4. Remember that factories generate income but reduce attractiveness.
5. Keep some money available for emergencies.
6. Repair damaged buildings.
7. Buy Disaster Shields before triggering disasters.
8. Plan construction times carefully.
9. Avoid filling all 16 tiles too quickly.
10. Monitor your building HP and daily income.

---

## 13. YOUR DEVELOPMENT STRATEGY

**STEP 1 – START SMALL**

Build houses and establish your population.

**STEP 2 – DEVELOP YOUR ECONOMY**

Construct farms, restaurants and factories to increase income.

**STEP 3 – IMPROVE YOUR CITY**

Build parks and luxury apartments to increase attractiveness.

**STEP 4 – PROTECT YOUR INVESTMENT**

Purchase Disaster Shields and repair damaged buildings.

**STEP 5 – KEEP EXPANDING**

Use your income to fill the available map and improve your city statistics.
